Things to do in Kennebunkport

Dozens of award-winning restaurants, boutiques and beaches — and Dock Square is a four-minute stroll from our door.

Enjoy Kennebunkport, Maine

Something for everyone

From shopping to fine dining to sailing, there’s something for everyone. History buffs can tour the homes of sea captains and shipbuilders, nature lovers can head out on a whale watching or kayaking trip, and antique collectors can find one-of-a-kind treasures in our boutiques.

Head a few miles up the coast to Cape Porpoise, a classic Maine fishing village with a stunning view of Goat Island Lighthouse, then on to Goose Rocks Beach — voted one of the top beaches in Maine by The Guardian.

Kennebunk & Gooserocks Beaches

Kennebunk Beach is a blissful place to relax — one of the most beautiful beaches in the area, with a playground and a lifeguard on duty. For soft sand and long walks, Gooserocks Beach fits the bill.

Dock Square

Bargain hunter or seaside browser? Dock Square has a wide variety of shops and chic boutiques, plus whale watching, sailing excursions and fine dining along this scenic stretch of Kennebunkport.

Goat Island Lighthouse

Established in 1833, Goat Island Lighthouse has been a boating landmark for generations. In summer you can visit by boat and get a personal tour from the lighthouse keepers.

More to discover

Attractions near the inn

Seasonal

Vinegar Hill Music Theatre

National, regional and local musical entertainment in an intimate, rustic barn setting throughout the summer and fall seasons.

Golf

Cape Arundel Golf Club

Drawing golf enthusiasts since 1896, with beautiful views and challenging holes for all skill levels.

History

First Families Museum

Two centuries of Kennebunkport history, from sea captains to presidents, with guided tours and a museum store.

Museum

Seashore Trolley Museum

The largest electric railway museum in the world, with vehicles gathered from nearly all of the nation’s major cities.

Nature

Blowing Cave

An opening in the rocks lets water spout up to 30 feet into the air before high tide. Prepare to get wet!

Harbor

Cape Porpoise Pier

See lobstering up close and watch fishermen haul traps in this charming tidal harbor.

Landmark

The Wedding Cake House

Often called the most photographed house in Maine, decorated in an ornate style unlike any other.

Drive

Walker’s Point

Drive Ocean Avenue for the Blow Holes, sweeping ocean vistas and a view of President George Bush’s summer home.

Wildlife

Rachel Carson National Wildlife Refuge

Protects salt marshes and estuaries for migratory birds. Open sunrise to sunset for birdwatching and self-guided tours.
