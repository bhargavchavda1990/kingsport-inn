Welcome

Park the car. Walk to the whole village.

King’s Port Inn is an intimate, classically appointed hotel in the heart of Lower Village Kennebunk. Surrounded by boutique shops, local restaurants, art galleries and historic attractions, you can explore the seaside village on foot or by trolley — or head offshore on a lobster boat or kayak.

We’re family owned and we appreciate our guests. Expect traditional New England ambiance, friendly service and exceptionally affordable rates.
