The hotel

Amenities at our Kennebunk hotel

King’s Port Inn offers relaxing, comfortable accommodations with traditional New England ambiance and exceptionally affordable rates — in a resort town known for its expensive hotels.

After a day exploring, relax in one of our 35 rooms, each with Wi-Fi, a flat-screen fiber optic cable TV, a mini-refrigerator and comfortable bedding. For extra privacy, space and convenience, upgrade to a room with a two-person Jacuzzi tub and gas fireplace.
